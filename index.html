Chainsaw Man

Remake of the kinetic typography of the promotional trailer of one of the volumes of the manga Chainsaw Man. The process of remaking the animation served me as a didactic resource to explain how to work with animated textures and moving typography.

TBHK

Particle animations I made with Stardust and CC Particle World as part of a personal practice animation on the manga Jibaku Shōnen Hanako-kun. The full animation with music is shown at the beginning of my tutorial on roll transitions.

Element 3D

Kinetic typography that I animated with Element 3D inside After Effects. I made an introductory tutorial about the plug-in.

Miku

Animation I made as a basis for a couple of class sessions I taught on the use of masks, precompositions, parents and "radio waves" within After Effects. I also did a complementary asynchronous tutorial on the latter via my channel.

Retro

Concept work for one of my past reels (video portfolio). The particle animation of the computer scene was made from scratch with Plexus.

2019

My 2019 audiovisual reel. It shows part of the kind of work I did while I was working full time in an agency and starting as a freelancer -mostly explainer animations-, it also contains some personal projects.

Hoshi to Harunezumi I

Personal animation based on the manga Hoshi to Harunezumi.

Hoshi to Harunezumi II

Personal animation based on the manga Hoshi to Harunezumi.

Shinsou

Animation of a vignette from the manga BNHA. The process to adapt the illustration for animation, and the animation itself, I explain it in a workshop on character animation in After Effects. In my YouTube channel I uploaded a timelapse of the animation.

Evangelion

Didactic resource for a camera typography animation class in After Effects. I also made a complementary asynchronous tutorial on the text distortion effect.

Instagram

Promotional sales reel I made with some animations for Instagram stories, from the time I worked as a 'full time' freelancer.

PUCP

Animation that I made as part of an introductory After Effects workshop that I gave on 10/30/21 for the students of the audiovisual communication specialty of the PUCP.

Flower Shower

Instructional resource I animated for an asynchronous tutorial on intermediate particle animation within After Effects.

Familiar

Short musical kinetic typography that I animated as a didactic resource for a class I taught about animated typography and camera. It's a couple of phrases from the chorus of the song "Familiar" by Agnes Obel -my favorite song in life!

Pride

Conceptual GIF that I made as part of the exercises of the course "Motion Beast", I had to create some animation inside of 4 squares using basic shapes. There are 4 parts that make up a single .gif

Nebula

Teaching resource I animated for an asynchronous tutorial on animating a nebula -and particles- inside After Effects.

山河令

Short GIF that I animated as a practice.

Higurashi

Kinetic musical typography that I animated as a didactic resource for a couple of classes I taught about typography in motion and camera in After Effects. The background montage was made with clips from the anime Higurashi no Naku Koro ni Gou (2020).

Restart wa Tadaima no Ato De

Personal practice animation based on the manga "リスタートはただいまのあとで" -Restart wa Tadaima no Ato De-. The complete animation is shown at the beginning of my asynchronous tutorial on 3D turns in After Effects.

Madoka

Teaching resource for a class I taught as an introduction to motion graphics animation in After Effects.

Space Girl

Instructional resource I animated for an asynchronous tutorial on a particular style of typography animation within After Effects.
